## 2. Two Zero-Fit, Bitwise-Exact Legal Scheduling/Placement Equivalences Overlooked in `draft001`

### 2.1 Equivalence 1 (Removes 2-Phase Flash Barrier & Quality Loss Without Any Bank): `B_FlashGU_HostDown` (+ Hot-`GU` Cache) on `DeepSeek`
- **Observation**: Treating native original `B` on `DeepSeek` (`14.43 GB` routed `W8`) as `100% Flash-resident` (`352` `GU` pages + host SwiGLU + `176` `Down` pages = 2 serial Flash phases and 6 private `Down` input uploads) leaves **`~5.8–6.3 GB` of the `8 GB` host DRAM completely idle**! Meanwhile, `NL` puts `3.47 GB` of approximated `GU` on Host and spills `4.81 GB` of `Down` (with private inputs!) to Flash—**the exact wrong split on `DeepSeek`**.
- **Exact Legal Placement `B_FlashGU_HostDown`**:
  - Pin **all `1,664` original canonical `W8 Down` matrices + `FP32` row scales (`4.812 GB`, or `4.831 GB` incl. `GU` scales)** in Host DRAM alongside `~1.53 GB` non-FFN + host-pinned `Q8_0` shared + dense `L0` (`~6.36 GB` subtotal, fitting inside **both `8.00e9 B` and `8 GiB`** with `~1.1–2.0 GB` left for KV/workspace + caching `150–300` hot `GU` pairs!).
  - Keep **only `Gate` and `Up` (`9.60 GB`, standard `H=2048` rows)** on **100% stock unmodified AiF** (using `LSB 28 μs` or `CSB/MSB 46 μs` under the exact same capacity contract as `C`).
- **Provable Equivalence & Architectural Impact**:
  1. **Bitwise identical to `B_allroutedcanonical` ($\Delta\text{NLL} \equiv 0$ everywhere)**: `GU` integer dot on stock AiF + Host `SwiGLU` + Host AVX2 `W8A8` `Down` (`17.30 MB/layer` at $K=6$, measured directly by `BENCH`'s `B` `Down` stage!) produces **bit-for-bit identical `F32` outputs** to `B`.
  2. **Eliminates the `GU -> Host -> Flash Down` round-trip barrier and all $K$ private Flash input uploads for FREE**: Stock AiF receives **one layer-shared `A8(x)` broadcast** (`2,048 B`), runs **one Flash phase (`GU`)**, and streams `(g_e, u_e)` (`67.6 KiB` total at $K=6$) to Host CPU, where `SwiGLU + Host Down` pipelines per expert as `GU` waves complete—requiring **zero `r=128` short-row hardware changes** and **zero `14.8 GB` offline bank**!
  3. **Sharpens `C_local`'s burden of proof**: Removing the 2-phase Flash barrier is **not** unique to local-affine compilation. `C_local` must specifically justify its `<= 2%` PPL loss and custom `r=128` PE/FIFO hardware by beating `B_FlashGU_HostDown` (and `B_HybridCache`, which caches `~680` full `8.67 MB` original experts in `~5.9 GB` DRAM and runs Host hits in parallel with stock-AiF misses!) via its `22×` smaller uncached Flash footprint (`16` pages vs `352` `GU` pages).

### 2.2 Equivalence 2 (Removes *In-Flash Short-Row PE* Necessity Using the Same `C_local` Bank): `C_Cache_DMA_CPU`
- **Observation**: [stage2_root_review_notes.md](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage2_root_review_notes.md#L30) mandated comparing *"ordinary map loading plus CPU execution, and in-flash execution of misses"*, yet [stage3_control_cost.protocol.draft001.json](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage3_control_cost.protocol.draft001.json#L113-L129) omits **`C_Cache_DMA_CPU`** from its explicit cost-window equations.
- **Exact Legal Schedule `C_Cache_DMA_CPU`**:
  - Store the exact `C_local` bank on a **stock commodity NVMe SSD (zero on-die PEs, zero `r=128` reset/FIFO hardware)** and cache hot `256 KiB` maps in the **same `~5.8 GB` free host DRAM** (`~22,000` maps $\approx 41\%$ of `DeepSeek`'s `53,248`-map bank; `~63%` of `OLMoE`'s bank).
  - Hits execute on Host CPU; $m_{\text{miss}} \in \{0 \dots K\}$ missed maps (`262,144 B` each) are ordinary-read (`28 μs` LSB / `46 μs` CSB-MSB + controller ECC), DMA'd over standard PCIe (`32.77 μs/map` payload at `8 GB/s`, `1,536 KiB = 196.6 μs` worst-case at $m_{\text{miss}}=6$, `65.5 μs` at $m_{\text{miss}}=2$), and multiplied on Host CPU via `BENCH`'s exact AVX2 `madd_epi16` `C_host` kernel (**100% bitwise identical outputs to `C_inflash`!**).

Because `C_local` shrinks active weights by **`33×`** (`1.57 MB` uncached; `0.52 MB` at 2 misses), commodity NVMe DMA + Host AVX2 (`pipelined across misses`, zero custom silicon) beats `C_inflash` whenever `C_inflash`'s extra short-row `PE + FIFO + setup + I32-drain` non-overlapped service exceeds `C_Cache_DMA_CPU`'s non-overlapped `W8-DMA (32.77 μs/miss @ 8 GB/s) + Host AVX2 GEMV` service!
