## 2. Streaming Labels, Common Teacher Budget, and Cryptographic Auditability Within `161 GB` Free Disk

Naively persisting `N × 2048` `FP32` dense targets (`242.7 MB/operator`) takes `62.1 GB` at `256` operators and `652.1 GB` at `2,688` operators (exceeding the `161 GB` free disk).    - Evaluate `canonical_expert(l, e, X)` once in RAM, yielding `Y_e[N, 2048]` (`242.7 MB` transient RAM) **and**, inside the exact same forward call at **zero extra GU integer MACs**, the canonical preactivations `g_e[N, I], u_e[N, I]` (`333 MB` Deep / `242 MB` OLM transient RAM).
   - **Immediate single-expert fits before freeing `Y_e, g_e, u_e`**:
     - Fit `C_local` (`A_{e,c}`, row-`W8` lowering, deployed `FIT` pass, exact `b_{Y,e,c}` residual mean) and per-expert `D_PE` (`q=512, W8`, reusing $\hat{W}_{e,c}$) using the exact frozen Stage-0/1 kernels.
     - Fit the prospective nonlinear control `F_projGU` ([nonlinear_baseline_design.sol.md](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/nonlinear_baseline_design.sol.md)): compute `eta=0` pooled OLS slopes `B_G,e, B_U,e` from `(g_e, u_e)` and `V`, calibrate `b_G,e,c, b_U,e,c`, and either run the single `canonical_W8_Down_e(qa, sa)` calibration pass immediately (`+1 H I` MACs, saving a future `2 H I = 37.8 T MAC` GU recomputation!) or save `(B_G,e, B_U,e, b_G,e,c, b_U,e,c)` plus `FP64` leaf target sums $\sum_{n \in I_c} y_{e,n}$ (`524 KB/expert`) for deferred zero-GU-query calibration.
   - **Exact sufficient statistics for coupled layer/cluster-shared output lifts (`MoLAE` / `EBC`) without storing `64 × Y_e` (`15.5 GB/layer`)**:

After `e = 63`, eigendecompose $M_{\mathcal{G}} \to U_{\mathcal{G}}$, project $B_{e,c} = U_{\mathcal{G}}^T \hat{W}_{e,c}$, lower to `W8`, and calibrate leaf output biases exactly via $b_{Y,e,c} = \frac{1}{N_c}\sum_{n \in I_c} y_{e,n} - \frac{1}{N_c}\sum_{n \in I_c} \hat{y}^{\text{deployed}}_e(x_n)$ (**zero second teacher pass**).
