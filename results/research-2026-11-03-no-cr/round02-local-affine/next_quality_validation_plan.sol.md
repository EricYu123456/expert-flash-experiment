## 2. Three distinct trajectory tests

1. **Same-token teacher-prefix replay:** feed identical externally fixed tokens into separate A/B/C/D contexts. Each method constructs its own hidden states, current routes and KV from those tokens. Compare next-token NLL/KL/logit agreement and route/fallback changes. Identical tokens do not imply identical hidden states/KV. No teacher KV is copied into a student.
2. **Local oracle at student states:** at selected current C/D inputs x_student, evaluate canonical f_e(x_student) and compare the substitute. This diagnoses approximation under distribution shift, including the correct current IDs/alpha. It is not f_e(x_teacher), not teacher-prefix logits, and not an end-to-end quality metric. Detached oracle outputs do not feed the student or replace its KV.
3. **True free-running:** each model uses its own logits/tokens/states/routes/KV; greedy tie policy and EOG stopping are fixed. Report generated-length distribution and quality/task outcomes. A fixed64step continuation that feeds EOG further is a stress diagnostic, not normal generation. A paired fixed-token workload may compare per-token service fairly; native end-to-end generation also reports actual completion length and total cost, avoiding speed claims from shorter/broken outputs.

Use exact natural live-alpha mixtures. Independent expert errors cannot be summed as squared norms to infer layer error; compare the actual weighted sum. Route divergence may change later task layout/cache/fallback and must enter each method's own resource trace.
