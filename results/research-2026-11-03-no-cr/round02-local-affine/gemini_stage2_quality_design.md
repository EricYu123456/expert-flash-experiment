## 0. Mandatory Mathematical Self-Corrections (Closing Stage-1 Loose Ends)

1. **Hadamard non-equalization & `amax` non-monotonicity (`[Proven]`)**: One deterministic signed Walsh-Hadamard $Q_r = \frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}H_r D_s$ equalizes diagonal variances **only** when $\Sigma$ is diagonal or in expectation over random signs; for non-diagonal conditional covariances (e.g., $\Sigma = \bigl[\begin{smallmatrix}1&0.5\\0.5&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr]$), $H_2 \Sigma H_2^T = \operatorname{diag}(1.5, 0.5)$ worsens dispersion, and row $[1,1] \mapsto [\sqrt{2}, 0]$ increases `amax` (not adopted).
2. **Retracting false `"iff eta = lambda = 0"` converse & distinguishing RRR eigen-objectives (`[Proven]`, per [stage1_root_review_notes.md](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage1_root_review_notes.md#L9-L11))**:
   - In my Stage-1 note, stating $M_{\text{target-exact}} = M_{\text{plug}}$ *"iff $\eta = \lambda = 0$"* had a **false `"only if"` converse**. Exact equality holds iff $\sum_c ((\tilde{Y}_c - \hat{Y}_c)^T W_c \hat{Y}_c + \hat{Y}_c^T W_c (\tilde{Y}_c - \hat{Y}_c)) = \mathbf{0}$: unregularized OLS is **sufficient, not necessary** (e.g., $\tilde{Y}_c = \mathbf{0}$, or an exact zero-residual pooled prior $\tilde{Y}_c = V_c \hat{A}_{\text{pool}}^T$ satisfies equality for any $\lambda > 0$).
   - Moreover, **two distinct eigen-problems** were conflated: Sol's $\sum_c p_c N_c G_c^\dagger N_c^T$ optimizes the **penalized** restricted shrink-to-pooled fit, whereas $M_{\text{target-exact}}$ optimizes the **unpenalized `FIT` SSE of projecting fixed already-fitted ridge predictions** $\hat{Y}_c U U^T$. Neither real-arithmetic theorem guarantees optimal lowered `W8/A8` error or downstream NLL. Also, all 8 primary `rev003` `VALID` points chose pooled $\eta = 0$, so `C=1` extra-stage ridge filtering did not explain their `VALID` gap; and any latent RMS floor uses $\max_{1 \le j \le q} \rho_j$, never assuming $\rho_1$ is largest.
3. **No universal low-rank / span lower bounds (`[Proven]`, per [stage2_quality_harness.protocol.draft.json](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage2_quality_harness.protocol.draft.json#L84-L92))**: Projecting onto $(I_H - U_q U_q^T)$ only bounds exact real-arithmetic combinations for **that single fixed $(U_q, b)$**; it never bounds alternative rank-$q$ subspaces $U'$, different biases $b'$, context-dependent bases $U_c$, or model NLL. Furthermore, if stored $U$ is span-estimated with a nonzero relative SVD cutoff or executed with `FMA` / row-scaled rounding that leaves $\operatorname{col}_{\mathbb{R}}(U)$, it is not even a strict lower bound on that stored kernel's SSE.

## 1. Assessment of the Bounded-Quality Stopping Rule (`PG-19 validation16`, $\delta = \ln 1.02$)

### 1.1 Epistemic Scope & Dilution Arithmetic (`Why Failure Is Decisive While Passing Is Weak`)
- **Exposure Dilution (`[Proven Counting]`)**: `DeepSeek` executes $26 \times 6 = 156$ routed expert slots + $26$ large ($I=2816$) shared branches per token; `OLMoE` executes $16 \times 8 = 128$ routed slots per token. Replacing only **4 operators per model** (`2` layers × `2/64` experts) touches **$\sim 0.5\%\text{–}2.5\%$ of total active FFN work**!
- **Why `lower simultaneous 95% band(C - B) > ln(1.02)` cleanly falsifies the adopted unguarded configuration**:
  - If replacing **$\sim 1\%$ of active FFN invocations** already degrades 16-book natural-text PPL by **statistically supported $> 2.0\%$** (blowing the *entire* full-model approximation budget on 4 operators out of 1,664 or 1,024!), full-model unguarded compilation (`~50×–100×` more replaced invocations) is practically untenable. And as [stage2_pg19_quality.protocol.draft.json](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage2_pg19_quality.protocol.draft.json#L438-L439) explicitly scopes it, rejection applies strictly to this concrete adopted unguarded `N120` configuration without claiming universal impossibility or rewriting Stage-0/1 inconclusive decode bands.
- **Why `upper band < ln(1.02)` is ONLY a weak necessary survival check**:
  - A `+0.012 nat` (`+1.2%` PPL) loss on `~1%` exposure passes `< ln(1.02)` yet linearly projects to catastrophic `+0.4–1.0 nat` degradation across all layers/experts—or could pass trivially if the 4 synthetic-`FIT`-selected experts rarely fire on 19th-century novels!

### 1.2 One Finite Pre-Payload Addition Needed Before Using Failure to Abandon Round 2
Is an unguarded `C_local_pilot` failure alone sufficient to walk away from the entire Round 2 candidate with **zero scientific regret**? **Almost, with ONE critical blind spot that has a cheap, finite pre-payload fix**:
- **The Blind Spot (`Domain + Position Extrapolation Without the Candidate's Own Pre-Read Guard`)**:
  - All 8 frozen local models were fitted **100% unguarded** on `corpus-300-v1` synthetic prompts at **token positions `0..135` only** (`min(128, N)` prefill + `8` decode), whereas `PG-19` scores **100% at positions `256..511`** of 19th-century books! Under RoPE/context-length + domain shift, even `5%` out-of-ball inputs (`||x - meanX_leaf||_2` outside the `FIT` leaf cloud) can trigger unbounded linear extrapolation spikes in an unguarded affine map, even though our candidate architecture from day one ([stage0_protocol.draft.json](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage0_protocol.draft.json#L96)) retains original Flash weights for a **pre-read distance guard**!
- **Finite Pre-Payload Correction (Zero Refitting, Zero New Hyperparameters, +1 Read-Only Arm)**:
  1. Add **one pre-frozen guarded arm `C_local_pilot_guard85`**: reuse each operator's **already-frozen Stage-0 `VALID` 85%-quantile `||x - meanX_leaf||_2` cutoff**, falling back to `B_pilotcanonical` inside the same Python worker when exceeded, and log achieved `PG-19` coverage $q_{\text{PG19}}$.
  2. **Pre-frozen abandonment rule**: If `C_local_pilot` (`100%`) breaches `lower band > ln(1.02)` **AND** (`C_local_pilot_guard85` **also** breaches `ln(1.02)` **OR** `guard85` achieved coverage on `PG-19` `pos 256..511` drops below `50%`, destroying single-phase coverage), **abandon this Round 2 path immediately and advance to Round 3 with zero ambiguity**. Conversely, if `100%` fails solely on rare OOD spikes while `guard85` holds `C - B << ln(1.02)` at high coverage (`>= 75%`), we avoid falsely killing a guarded compiler over an unguarded outlier!
  3. **Exposure & In-Range Position Telemetry (Zero Extra Forward Passes)**: Log per-book pilot hit counts, routed weight exposure $f_{\text{exp}} = \frac{1}{256 L_{\text{routed}}}\sum_{t,l,k \in \text{pilot}} \alpha_{t,l,k}$, exposure-scaled shift $\Delta_{\text{NLL}} / \max(f_{\text{exp}}, \epsilon)$, and unscored warm-chunk `pos 64..127` NLL/distance diagnostics (already computed by `batch=ubatch=128` prefill logits!) to separate `pos 0..135 -> 256..511` shift from intrinsic operator error.

## 2. Audit of Causal, Numerical, and Callback Confounds

1. **`B_pilotcanonical - source` Transformation vs `C - B` Approximation (`Double Quantization on OLMoE`)**:
   - `DeepSeek` source is `Q8_0` (32-weight block scales + `Q8_1` block activations), while `OLMoE` source is **`Q4_K_M`** (`~4.5–5.5` bit K-quants + `Q8_K` block activations). `B_pilotcanonical` dequantizes those `GGUF` rows and re-quantizes to **per-row `W8` + whole-vector `A8`** (`double quantization` on `OLMoE`, plus whole-vector `I=1024/1408` dynamic `A8` outlier sensitivity vs 32-wide block `A8`!).
   - *Implication*: Using `C - B` as the primary criterion rightly isolates `C`'s surrogate error relative to its exact training teacher `B`, while co-reporting `B - source` and `C - source` is mandatory so a large `B - source` degradation is never hidden behind a small `C - B` gap (or vice versa via Stein/ridge smoothing).
2. **Causal Asymmetry Between First vs Second Pilot Layer Under Teacher-Forced Independent Trajectories (`[Proven Graph Property]`)**:
   - In `OLMoE` (`L0, L8`) and `DeepSeek` (`L1, L13`, since `L0` is untouched dense!), under teacher-forced ground-truth token feeding (`j = 0..511`), **the first pilot layer (`OLMoE L0`, `DeepSeek L1`) receives BIT-IDENTICAL inputs $x$, router IDs, and $\alpha$ across `source`, `B`, `C`, and `D` at all 512 positions**!
   - Only layers **downstream** of `L0`/`L1` (specifically `L8` and `L13`, via perturbed residual stream + attention KV caches of intermediate layers) experience trajectory/KV/route divergence—and even `L0`/`L1` tiny perturbations (`B` vs `source` or `C` vs `B`) can flip discontinuous top-$K$ boundary ties in downstream untouched layers `L2..L26`. Keeping independent fresh contexts/KV per arm is the **only** causally coherent choice, while recognizing that 2-layer teacher-forced replay suppresses cascading 26-layer state drift and free-running token divergence.
3. **Raw-Text Windowing & Cross-`ubatch` Off-By-One Alignment (`batch = ubatch = 128`)**:
   - Starting after the first `\n` past Unicode midpoint in hard-wrapped (72-char) Project Gutenberg text typically lands mid-sentence and prepends `<BOS>` (`add_special=true`); leaving `j = 0..255` as unscored context warm-up cleanly insulates `j = 256..511` from the mid-sentence `<BOS>` transient (and smallest book `42306` at `44,891 B` still has `~5,000+` suffix tokens $\gg 512$).
   - **Critical Indexing Check**: With `512` tokens split into **4 `llama_decode` calls of `128` tokens**, target token `j = 256` must be scored against **`logits[255]`**—the **last row (`t = 127`) of ubatch #2 (`128..255`)**, NOT row 0 of ubatch #3! Verify this boundary explicitly in synthetic unit tests before data release.
4. **C++ Callback & Thread-Pool Numerical Traps**:
   - **Buffer lifetime**: Copy `x = up_exps->src[1]` and `ids = up_exps->src[2]` immediately inside `up_exps` (`ask=false`), because `ggml-alloc` can recycle `ffn_norm` (`cur`) buffer memory once `gate_exps`/`up_exps` finish, *before* `down_exps` completes! Verify bitwise equality against `ffn_norm` output during the `source-observer` gate.
   - **Per-thread `MXCSR`**: On x86_64 Linux, `MXCSR` (`RC=RN`, `FTZ=0`, `DAZ=0`) is thread-local; verify `MXCSR` **inside every C++/OpenMP and Python BLAS worker thread**, not solely on the main process thread, and enforce strict sequential request/response framing so `ggml` 8-thread compute and Python 8-thread BLAS never spin concurrently.

## 3. Path to a Real NAND/AiF Contribution vs Exact Stopping Conditions

### 3.1 What Minimal Next Evidence Could Make This a Genuine NAND/AiF Paper?
Notice a crucial empirical clue already visible in [stage2_quality_harness.protocol.draft.json](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage2_quality_harness.protocol.draft.json#L41-L602): to reach `VALID_loss` `0.0167 – 0.1791` (comparable to `C_local_pilot`'s `0.0147 – 0.1667` at **`r = 128`** = `256 KiB` active/expert), `D_factor_pilot` (`PE_RRR`/`G_RRR`) required **`q = 512`** (`1.06–2.00 MiB` active/expert = **`4.25×–8×` more active coefficients/MACs** and **`5.79 decimal GB`** routed footprint on `DeepSeek`)! Meanwhile, per [stage1_root_review_notes.md](file:///home/ericyu/expert_flash_experiment/results/research-2026-11-03-no-cr/round02-local-affine/stage1_root_review_notes.md#L36), `OLMoE`'s **uncompressed original `W8` model** has a known floor of **`~6.80 decimal GB`**, below `8 GB` before workspace/long-KV!

Consequently, a credible **AiF/NAND systems paper** beyond `Flash-Jacobian` (mechanistic GPU local low-rank probes with `13.96 GB` region-specific input factors) and `MoLAE` (shared output lifts) exists **if and only if** the next experiments establish **all three** of the following minimal pillars:
1. **All-Routed Quality Survival**: Partial screen (`C` or `C_guard85` at high coverage) passes `PG-19 validation16`, **and** streaming compilation of **all routed experts** (`26×64` `DeepSeek`, `16×64` `OLMoE`) preserves held-out `PG-19 test32`, zero-shot task accuracy (`HellaSwag-256`), and free-running generation vs `B_allroutedcanonical` and `source` within the preset `2%` budget.
2. **Failure or Memory/Latency Inferiority of Host-Fittable Factorizations (`Defeating MoLAE / Shared-U / RRR`)**:
   - Compact Host-fittable factorizations (`Layer/Group-Shared U_{g,q}`, `PE_RRR`/`G_RRR` at `q <= 256`) **fail** the `2%` quality budget, while rank-inflated `D_factor(q=512)` either **overflows the inclusive `8e9 B / 8 GiB` Host DRAM budget** on `DeepSeek` (`~7.4–8.0+ GB` inclusive) or runs **slower on 8-core CPU DRAM bandwidth (`~164 MB/token` `U` reads)** than modeled no-CR AiF `g4` ordinary-read waves (`28 us` LSB, explicit FIFO/post/shared-branch DAG).
