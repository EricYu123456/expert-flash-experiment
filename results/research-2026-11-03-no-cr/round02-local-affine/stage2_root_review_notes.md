## Qualifications to Gemini's Stage2 design note

Gemini's independent note is preserved as authored. Its broad concerns about domain/position shift, live trajectories, intervention dilution, pre-read guards and source-versus-canonical references are useful. The following claims are not adopted:

- Four pilot operators per model do not establish an observed0.5–2.5% exposure. Actual hit counts, alpha mass and compute-weighted work must be measured separately. Deep's I2816 shared branch has twice a routed I1408 expert's coefficient work; a branch count is not a MAC fraction. OLM selected weights are not necessarily normalized to sum1. Any alpha exposure fraction needs the actual total alpha denominator.
- Neither partial ΔNLL divided by exposure nor multiplying it by50–100 predicts all-layer loss. Signed errors, routing changes, attention/KV evolution and downstream nonlinearities can interact. A supported pilot regression can reject that concrete pilot configuration under its preset budget; it does not mathematically prove all-layer catastrophe or every guarded variant impossible. A partial pass is likewise weak evidence for full compilation.
- The first modified layer's inputs can agree across fixed-token arms because all preceding computations remain equal, assuming the same deterministic numerical execution. Later modified and unmodified layers still respond to changed hidden/KV states. Teacher-forced observable tokens do not pin those hidden states and do not suppress propagation through the remaining network.
- q512 was one of two tested factor ranks, not a proved required minimum. No q256 deployment result exists. The target footprint is an inclusive known floor until workspace/cache/KV/source-shared contracts close. A proposed smaller host budget is only a separately labeled sensitivity case, not a substitute for the main AiF target or a way to exclude its original-host control.
- Guard85 coverage below50% is not a valid standalone failure rule. Even partial coverage can reduce work, and per-expert guard coverage does not determine the joint all-selected-expert fallback DAG. No independence or q^K assumption is allowed. A guarded arm, if added, must use a frozen actual map/cutoff and report complete fallback cost; coverage alone cannot establish loss of engineering value.
- The numerical size of a book object does not prove its tokenizer suffix contains512 tokens. That check occurs after authorized acquisition, with the prospective short-book rule; it never silently substitutes another book.
- Full vocabulary callback logits, target indexing across chunk boundaries (target256 uses logits255), owned up-node snapshots, exact original slot-order/live-alpha application and independent fresh replay/free-run contexts are required numerical checks. Sample logit equality is not a population equivalence theorem.
- Statements that an AiF paper exists “if and only if” a particular evidence list passes are not theorems. A defensible contribution needs meaningful new quality/resource/latency evidence beyond conditional-affine priors, with honest hardware limits and strong controls. The specific research path can be abandoned after a valid bounded experimental failure without claiming a universal impossibility result.
