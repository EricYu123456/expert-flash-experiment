## First counterexamples: the objective may not need high-order learning

Under uniform K-subset routing among E experts, a group used by d experts is active with probability `1 - C(E-d,K)/C(E,K)`. At fixed degree sequence and equal page cost, rearranging which experts share which group does not change expected page cost. A degree/overlap penalty can therefore be sufficient in that case. For K=2, inclusion-exclusion stops at pairwise coactivation exactly; no higher-order cohort term exists. For actual K6/K8, higher-order structure may matter, but must beat a pairwise/coactivation control rather than assume it.

At fixed per-expert support cardinality, minimizing the pure union penalty tends to concentrate everyone on the same few groups. This can collapse the functional bank to a shared nonlinear bottom plus distinct Down heads. Quality must justify that concentration; no diversity/quality lower bound follows from the regularizer. A generic load-balancing penalty can oppose the desired reuse, so the final recipe must state what is optimized rather than hide contradictory objectives.

### All-pair replicas can defeat the fixed-unique-pair wave story

Under **Root's own conditional** one-wave/two-T128-GU-group geometry, capacity can purchase every static pairing in advance. Store one legal four-plane same-wordline copy for each unordered pair of pool groups. For G32, there are496 physical pair copies, each1MiB globally across16chips, so496MiB GU per layer. For any observed active set with u≥2 groups, choose arbitrary disjoint pairs from that set, using the stored pair copies; for odd u, read one pair with an inactive partner and ignore its outputs. This uses `ceil(u/2)` waves under the stated geometry. No online page programming, free bin packing, multiple unrelated inputs or additional host cache copies is assumed. Addresses, pair selection/tags, scales/ECC, extra outputs and physical capacity are charged. Ordinary load receives exactly the same replicas and schedule.

This construction removes the supposed necessity of the **unique** physical-pair arrangement and its almost16-wave independent-support example.
