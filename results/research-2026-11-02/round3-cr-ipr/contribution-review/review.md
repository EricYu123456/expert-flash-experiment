# Contribution review: operand domains and executable NAND lowering

**A generic compiler's ability to represent this layout, or to replay its identical graph, does not establish that published prior work already supplied this lowering.** It establishes representability and removes an additional speedup claim against that identical execution. There may remain a backend-specific lowering or problem-formulation contribution. The present evidence does not yet establish its novelty, native feasibility, or publication readiness.

This is a bounded comparison of saved primary versions and the repository's earlier implementation. It is not a priority clearance or an exhaustive literature search. The new shared/private partition's matched full-FFN evaluation is still pending; no new speedup is assumed here.

## What an exact generic tie establishes

| Observation | Valid conclusion | Conclusion it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| A generic IR accepts the supplied row lists, commands and schedules | The mapping is expressible. It does not need a separate execution engine. | That a prior publication identified the transformation, its constraints, or its benefit. A general ILP can encode many subsequently formulated problems. |
| The proposed lowering is installed as a pass, then the generic backend emits the same graph | The implementations are aliases. Credit lies in the added pass, legality contract or cost model, if those are new. | A speedup over the alias, or evidence that the unchanged earlier compiler already performed the pass. |
| An unchanged earlier mapper, with capabilities it already possessed, independently reaches the same legal solution under the same objective and search budget | There is no demonstrated residual mapping/search advantage for the proposed heuristic. | Automatically that a newly established hardware contract or newly characterized failure mode was already known. Those require separate attribution. |
| A prior method/source actually specifies the same semantic transformation and backend constraints | That specific claimed transformation is anticipated, regardless of whether its implementation syntax differs. | That every stricter backend realization, integrated verifier or new empirical finding is also anticipated without comparison. |

The earlier audit's statement that a matched generic compiler removes a mapping-algorithm claim is defensible in this narrow sense. Treating generic expressibility as a blanket rejection of the whole systems question would be too strong. Conversely, renaming an ordinary grouping/replication control as a new lowering would be too strong in the other direction.

## Where a concrete contribution could remain

The broad observation that common inputs permit sharing while private intermediate inputs constrain placement is already prior. A possible narrower contribution is a **reusable lowering of operand-sharing equivalence classes to a particular native command language, with checkable semantic and resource contracts**. It would need to decide which ready row bands can share one input context, where separate private jobs are mandatory, and which static supports/command boundaries make that plan executable.

Here, “same input” must mean the same complete quantized input bytes, dimensions and input scale under the fixed arithmetic contract, rather than a casual tensor-name match. Rows retain their original weights/scales and logical output identity. Private Down waits for complete expert GU at the host and full activation/A8; distinct private vectors are not interleaved through an undocumented context mechanism.

The useful backend-specific difficulty is joint legality: complete-row input phase across selected pages; one-block commands and actual resident addresses; plane-source order and explicit overfetch/drop; one current row context; two weight slots per plane; copy/overwrite deadlines with one ECC path; chip ownership through output drain; bounded continuous-drain FIFO; and the same allocated-capacity budget. A compact descriptor is insufficient if any of these obligations is absent.

There are already substantive negative witnesses: two-page shared interruptions can switch owner inside a row; a fully occupied twelve-wave stream can fail at500ns copy while a longer thirteen-wave stream passes; arbitrary private-Down concatenation changes the operand; and spare pages on only two planes produce a slower replica. These make the legality layer meaningful. They do not prove a new theorem or that no earlier compiler handled comparable contracts on another backend.

For the new shared/private support partition, the precise mechanism to test is separating always-active shared-Down work from routed-Down collisions while retaining common-input GU aggregation. Three routed g4 groups plus dedicated shared g4 are a standard resource-partition witness. The21/21/21+one-cold-expert choice is relevant under an explicit one-block constraint: candidate GU128WL plus21 routed bands×3WL reaches191WL. Under the original two-block budget,22/21/21 is also admissible; excluding it would weaken the baseline. Choosing the least frequent exception is profiling/grouping, not a new solver. Any gain that also appears for RR under the same partition belongs to the shared/private support decision, not to the special GU stencil.

| Claim class | Current defensible position |
|---|---|
| New phenomenon | A backend-specific causal interaction among CR startup, private-job fanout, shared contention and finite-buffer feasibility is a candidate finding. Common-input reuse, cold amortization and load partition individually are prior. The new partition's benefit remains to be measured fairly. |
| New problem/formulation | Potentially the precise operand-domain-to-executable-stream optimization with these contracts. Must define inputs, decisions, legal native programs and scope; do not call MoE placement or hybrid parallelism new. |
| New solver | Not supported. Group labels, swaps, cold-expert exceptions, standard DP/ILP and replicated declustering are existing methods. A generic alias provides no additional algorithmic gain. |
| New lowering/executability | Plausible narrow systems contribution relative to the inspected sources, if generalized and independently validated. The current collection of witnesses/checkers is evidence toward it, not priority proof. |
| New hardware | None demonstrated or claimed. If a required source selector/drop/CR continuation is unavailable, this is a conditional backend assumption; a numerical/address proof does not create that hardware capability. |

## Smallest evidence package that would change the assessment

1. **State and validate the backend contract.** For an existing-hardware claim, obtain documented native support or a validated implementation for the required addresses, continuation, source selection, copy timing, output semantics and FIFO. Otherwise frame the result as conditional systems/compiler analysis. Expose the28us phase split uncertainty and implicit assumption that the PE sustains the6.4GB/s ECC stream. Neither CPU arithmetic nor a consistent event model establishes these device facts.
2. **Deliver one general pass and one independent checker.** Accept an operation DAG plus input-equivalence/scales metadata and capacities; emit actual row/address/command/ownership/FIFO certificates or reject the plan. Show nontrivial second shapes/routes and the illegal counterexamples. Do not present a hand-coded G4 stencil or21-expert special case as a general compiler. Verification frameworks themselves have prior; the contribution must be this concrete lowering and its demonstrated necessity.
3. **Complete the matched attribution experiment.** Give candidate, RR and fixed-source GU controls the same routed/shared-Down support menu, learned-label budget, scheduler and one-/two-block budgets. Include22/21/21 under two blocks, old asymmetric controls, and standard same-capacity replicas. Compare unchanged earlier mapping, generic+new lowering and exact aliases explicitly. A tie with the latter is expected; a tie with independently retuned earlier capabilities narrows the contribution to contract/model findings.
4. **Show useful generality and practical cost.** Freeze choices before new route evaluation; report per-prompt mean/tail/regressions, capacity/payload, physical row/scale equivalence, full host GU→activation→private Down→combine, metadata/selection/command/transfer costs, and FIFO/copy/phase sensitivity. Obtain at least a small independent workload/shape check rather than relying solely on repeatedly reused TRAIN. No fixed percentage is a publication criterion; the current TRAIN gains alone are insufficient.

A careful prospective claim is: “We formulate and implement operand-domain-aware whole-row lowering for a specified AiF-like native contract, producing independently checkable physical-stream and finite-resource certificates, and evaluate the resulting merge/support choices against equally capable mappings.” That claim remains conditional until the pass, contract evidence and fair partition evaluation exist. “First common-input fusion,” “first asymmetric sharding,” “new grouping/replication solver,” “unmodified AiF supports this,” and “generic expressibility proves prior anticipation” are all unsupported.
