This appendix discusses the design rationale of NVLLM, its intended
deployment scenarios, and its broader implications for memory-

A.1     Why a 3D NAND-Centric Architecture?
NVLLM is motivated by a simple observation: as models continue
to grow, the memory system increasingly determines the overall
efficiency of edge AI inference. In this context, many architec-
tural variants are foreseeable. One could imagine more aggressive
forms of in-storage execution, deeper coupling between Flash and
programmable accelerators, or broader mappings of Transformer
operators into the NAND subsystem. NVLLM does not rule out
these possibilities. Instead, it deliberately chooses a narrower and
more concrete first step.
