# Physical pool focused primary-source screen

Scope: prior/device evidence and candidate judgment only, started 2026-11-02 21:08 UTC. No model weights/activations, numerical candidate probe, or candidate code. AiF paper Table 2 28/46/46 µs for be-enc LSB/CSB/MSB is **single-logical-page** evidence; it is not evidence that two MLC logical pages can be jointly read in 46 µs. Numerical ratios based on that joint value are hypotheses, not measurements.

## 2026-11-02 21:09 UTC — multi-bit cell read is an existing primitive, measured boundaries

- [US9851899B2, Figs. 14–15 and claims 5–6](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9851899B2/en) describes a TLC cell's LSB/CSB/MSB sequentially sensed into main/cache/auxiliary latches before their sequential output; three latches hold the three bits, and the document calls the operation one-shot read. This is strong direct prior for same-cell multi-logical-page capture. It does **not** by itself establish simultaneous comparisons, that the three references share a single BL precharge, discharge only once, any MLC timing number, AiF command support, or ECC-before-in-die-compute. Its pipelining example overlaps different devices' read and output.
- [Li et al., IEEE TCAS-II 2014 §§II–III](https://sites.ecse.rpi.edu/~tzhang/pub/UECTCASII2014.pdf) explains that MLC lower/upper pages use one/two reference sensing cycles respectively; recovering both physical bits requires three cycles in the described mapping. On sub-22nm commercial MLC chips, separate lower/upper sensing latencies measured 41/55 µs; no joint latency is reported. More error-prone upper pages require stronger coding or other protection; the authors' cross-page concatenated-ECC solution sometimes requires an additional lower-page read, not a free reliability fix. These technologies/timings cannot be substituted into AiF or prove pseudo-MLC joint performance.
- [Macronix AN0268, pp. 5–7](https://www.mxic.com.tw/Lists/ApplicationNote/Attachments/2117/AN0268V2-Improving%20NAND%20Throughput%20with%20Two-Plane%20and%20Cache%20Operations.pdf) distinguishes Data and Cache registers and pipelines array sensing of the next page with serial transfer of the previous page. It uses `00h ... 30h`, then `31h`; tRCBSY ~3 µs for transfer to cache in the documented SLC example. This cross-page transfer overlap is not a single same-WL multi-reference read and does not establish an AiF internal compute overlap.
- [Luo & Peleato, Multi-page read for NAND flash](https://engineering.purdue.edu/~bpeleato/multi-page%20read.pdf) uses the term *multi-page read* for selecting multiple **different wordlines** and producing a combined bitwise result; it is not evidence that both MLC logical pages on one wordline can be read jointly at a given latency. Name collisions must be checked against circuit/command details.

## 2026-11-02 21:10 UTC — strongest direct joint-command and compression prior

- [IBM US20160118110A1, Figs. 5B, 8, 10](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20160118110A1/en) describes combining addresses of same-WL MLC LSB and MSB in a single read command, decoding the cell state and capturing both outputs into separate page buffers. It also mentions TLC/QLC and full/half-page combinations. This is directly applicable prior to the claimed *joint command and two page buffers*, even if the proposed use is later fed to AiF PEs. It does not disclose a measured operating point, actual three-voltage circuit timing, ECC, in-flash MAC use, or proof that multiple reference decisions occur physically simultaneously. Patent title and claims are not device characterization.
- [Dong et al., IEEE TCAS-I 2013 §§III–VI](https://sites.ecse.rpi.edu/~tzhang/pub/LatencyTCASI2013.pdf) already proposes transparent intra-page lossless flash-data compression to create ECC space, and on-the-fly table-based decompression; it additionally compresses soft-sensing output to reduce controller transfer and estimates simple Huffman/RLE hardware. This is not weight-specific fused PE execution or a measurement of a streaming INT8 codec. Distinguish its sensing-output encoding from compressing stored weights to reduce physical page count. Its silicon estimate is process- and throughput-specific, not the candidate's die-area cost.
- [ZipMoE ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/yang26ds.html) gives actual lossless MoE compression and cache-affinity scheduling; it does not establish in-NAND PE decode or INT8 expert-specific savings for this workload. [ZipServ §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17435v1) explicitly fuses lossless decoder and GEMM, with a fixed-length, bit-map architecture designed for GPU register/Tensor-Core use and author [open-source implementation](https://github.com/QuiverDance/ZipServ_ASPLOS26). Its redundancy and results are mainly BF16 exponent distributions, not an INT8 compression guarantee; it is strong direct prior to generic load-compressed/compute-decompressed co-design.
- [Kim et al. JSSC 2021](https://people.ece.umn.edu/groups/VLSIresearch/papers/2021/JSSC21_NAND.pdf) demonstrate multi-level weight storage and bit-serial neural MAC on a NAND-inspired 65nm embedded-flash test chip; this is analog current integration with a very different array and circuit, not a digital AiF two-page sensed-weight implementation. The authors explicitly warn that their process/array differs significantly from real high-density 3D NAND.

## 2026-11-02 21:11 UTC — INT8 fused decoder prior and AiF resource collision

- [HardCompress ISQED 2021 §III](https://www.isqed.org/English/Archives/2021/ISQED_2021_Papers/pdf/4A-3-208.pdf) studies quantized 8-bit neural weights, lossless dictionary coding and hardware decompression per systolic array column feeding MACs at runtime. This is strong direct architectural prior to a generic small inline INT8 decoder, although not to AiF, MoE, or a 256-KiB flash-stream physical layout. The paper's CNN compression ratios are not evidence for INT8 LLM MoE weights.
- [Go et al. IEEE Access 2023 §III–IV](https://people.cs.ksu.edu/~amunir/documents/publications/journal/Munir_NMT_IEEEAccess_2023.pdf) implements quantized-weight Huffman decoding in near-memory hardware with 128-byte chunks, bitmap, codebook, multiple concurrent decoders and buffered PE output. Verilog synthesized at 32 nm and 500 MHz gives 8.7 cycles/weight/decoder on average, and 1,408 parallel decoders in their HBM/GPU NMT configuration. Different data, technology, hardware targets, and overlap mean these figures cannot be directly treated as AiF cost; they disprove the general claim that entropy decode near a neural PE is new, and illustrate the throughput/area tradeoff.
- AiF §4.4 ([paper DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3695053.3731073), local `paper/aif.txt`) says three data latches plus a cache latch exist per TLC PB, and reuses **two** data latches as input-vector buffers specifically because its LSB parameter read needs only one sensing latch. Joint multi-reference reads must establish actual temporary/captured-result latch lifetimes while retaining the inputs; simply counting available latches without an access schedule is invalid. A small new input buffer, latch, alternate dataflow or sequential handling may be required; which is minimal remains unproven.
- AiF Fig. 13 under its stated 4K P/E + one-year retention characterization plots maximum bit errors/1 KiB for its 1/3/3 encoding as LSB 9, CSB 69, MSB 66. ECCLITE corrects up to 10 bits/1 KiB. These measurements show why copying regular be-enc CSB/MSB to an in-flash compute path is not automatically covered by ECCLITE; 4-state pseudoMLC changes the state distribution and distances, so its actual error distribution cannot be assumed to equal either those upper-page values or the LSB value. A retention/endurance/ecc measurement or robust demonstrated model is required.

## 2026-11-02 21:12 UTC — four-state TLC precedent and more demanding INT8 precedent

- [US20260010286A1 Fig. 5–6](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20260010286A1/en) explicitly obtains four allowed states from a TLC eight-state cell by making one of three written bits a deterministic function of the other two, and contrasts its state distributions with normal MLC. It proposes wider margins and redundant-bit recovery; these are patent assertions, not measured AiF pseudoMLC joint-read tR or 4K-P/E/one-year RBER. Its discussion inconsistently describes `XP=LP` as following UP in adjacent sentences, so recover the state-to-page mapping from an unambiguous figure and independent physical validation before implementation. It also means four-state-on-TLC is not a novel general mechanism.
- [Gao et al. ACM TOS 2022 §7.2.7 and Table 4](https://storage.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/papers/tos22reprogramming.pdf) study a conventional 3D-TLC SSD simulated as 2-bit MLC mode, with the resulting one-third loss of capacity relative to full TLC; their 8.5% average read-latency reduction is an SSD RAID workload result in simulation, not a measured same-WL two-page tR. The paper separately verifies reprogram feasibility on real 3D TLC; do not mislabel the simulated MLC RAID results as real joint-command measurements.
- The two weight pages need not be high/low bits of the same INT8 value: one can map **two ordinary independent INT8 byte-stream pages** into the two logical pages of each selected WL, retrieve each exactly, and run the existing INT8 MAC twice. This is a simpler necessary matched baseline with no bit-plane unpack/shift accumulation. A paired significance-bit layout can instead support progressive precision, but changes arithmetic and needs explicit reconstruction. Either layout still has the ECC, latch lifetime, tR and capacity questions.
- [WACT, Electronics 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/15/17/3811) designs lossless INT8 weight/activation coding with software-frozen per-tensor policy, multicodec Rice modes and RAW fallback; a memory-side decoder reconstructs INT8 before an unchanged NPU buffer/MAC. It includes aligned metadata, held-out bit-exact full-model reinjection and representative RTL packet verification. This is edge NPU/SRAM and CNN rather than NAND MoE, but a very close prior and high bar for generic streaming decoder claims. [Mao et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01384) already examine lossless compression of quantized LLM weights to reduce offloaded inference data movement. [EntroPack 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34185v1) uses independently decodable rANS tiles with fused GPU decode/reconstruction and INT8 outputs, but applies lossy lattice quantization before entropy coding and so is not a bit-exact W8 preservation result.

## 2026-11-02 21:13 UTC — direct single-precharge physical patent

- [US9613704B2, description of Fig. 8E around `VR1`, `VR2`, `VR3`](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9613704B2/en) explicitly states that its four-state MLC read uses gradually increased reference voltages and **one BL precharge for three BL discharges**, and says that the MSB and LSB logical page data from the same selected MLC WL are output. It requires the described HiNAND hierarchical-bitline structure, capacitor/temporary registers and data-flipping; it is patent disclosure, not a measured commercial AiF operating point. Thus even same-WL, single-precharge, multi-reference sensing is disclosed prior; it does not supply a numerical joint tR, AiF latch compatibility, ECC guarantee, or direct in-flash MoE deployment. Its multi-WL aspects are an additional distinction and should not be presented as the proposed no-cross-WL technique.
- [Same-WL ramp/time-sensing research proposal](https://www.preprints.org/frontend/manuscript/bcd5f72122007a7cb224d59f5561b806/download_pub) also describes one-precharge time encoding via a ramped wordline voltage and a per-column timing latch/counter (or ADC). It is a circuit proposal with explicitly added analog/time machinery and illustrative distributions, not a measured 3D NAND result or a demonstrated small AiF modification.

## 2026-11-02 21:14 UTC — controls and compression accounting

- [ZipMoE §§2.2–2.3, 4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21198v1) reports actual DeepSeekV2-Lite and Qwen1.5-MoE measurements on Jetson + Samsung SSD, but its basic representation exploits **BF16 exponent** redundancy, split exponent and sign/mantissa chunks, plus CPU decompression and cache-affinity scheduling. It is a very strong MoE/flash software prior, not empirical evidence for our per-row INT8 distribution or die-side throughput. Its own scheduling already jointly overlaps loading and decompression; a new scheduler must exceed that general principle at the NAND geometry level.
- [Mao et al. Tables II–III](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.01384v2) report substantially different post-quantization entropy compression ratios across OPT models for tensor-wise SmoothQuant and channel-wise LLM.int8(). These are model- and quantizer-dependent; changing quantization to improve the entropy while claiming exact equality to the old INT8 reference is invalid. That paper's GPT-2 Table I labels appear inconsistent with its own prose about per-tensor vs per-channel; do not rely on the disputed directional row labels.
- An independently fenced 256-KiB stream that by itself occupies 16 × 16-KiB pages over four planes has four ideal ordinary-read waves; at most 12 occupied pages fit in three, so this *specific fenced case* needs at least 25% net byte saving for a whole-wave reduction. This is not a universal compression threshold: multiple ready streams may sometimes share empty planes in a legal co-schedule, and long unfenced extents can accumulate fractional savings across pages. Whether independent expert commands may do so depends on physical layout, chip command/concurrency support, barriers and routing; no such capability or saving has been measured here.
- For lossless compressed data, state precisely whether ECC protects compressed physical bytes and is performed before entropy decoding; one damaged variable-length prefix can corrupt subsequent outputs until a restart. Count parity, frame/codebook offsets, terminal state, tail padding, incompressible/raw escape and actual 4-plane occupancy. A large restart reduces duplicated framing/padding but limits parallel decode unless additional independent substreams or checkpoints are provided; those have nonzero metadata and area.

## 2026-11-02 21:14 UTC — additional integrated-memory priors

- [WO2024238425A2 Figs. 8, 10–13](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024238425A2/en) describes digital quantized neural weights in multilevel NAND cells, sequential reference comparisons to identify cell state and an in-die MAC using dynamic inputs. Its worked example is a 4-bit/16-state cell with up to 15 references; it can cease sensing individual cells after identification to save power. This is not an AiF two-logical-page joint read, not a 4-state 46-µs measurement, and not a demonstration of die/ECC cost, but the broad multilevel-digital-weight-plus-in-flash-MAC concept is already disclosed.
- [US20230071837A1 Fig. 9](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230071837A1/en) describes a DNN within a NAND memory-device system whose compressed weights include optional Huffman coding with LUT-based reconstruction; its DNN's task is NAND read-voltage estimation, not MoE inference or user-model in-flash PE execution. This further limits any claim of being first to combine NAND and compressed neural weights.
- [US20230037575A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230037575A1/en) discusses lossless INT8 prefix/LUT/Huffman and selective raw compression in an integrated accelerator device network. It recognizes that prefix overhead can make encoded blocks larger and that an adaptive selection is necessary. It is a systems-format prior, not evidence of a four-plane NAND sensing-wave scheduler.

## 2026-11-02 21:15 UTC — compressed extent packing already studied in SSDs

- [IBM INFLOW 2014](https://research.ibm.com/publications/compression-and-ssd-where-and-how) studies in-SSD compression granularity and placement of compressed data in flash pages; [Li et al. FAST 2015](https://www.usenix.org/conference/fast15/technical-sessions/presentation/li) explicitly distinguishes compressing data within a page from increasing the number of logical sectors packed in that page. Thus page packing and changed granularity are old mechanisms, although neither source establishes this candidate's selected-expert die-PE execution.
- [CCZNS HPCA 2025 §§II–III](https://yingjia-wang.github.io/papers/cczns-final.pdf) is a still closer systems prior: hardware compression/decompression inside SSDs, byte-granular compressed chunk packing across flash pages, host-maintained chunk index, length/offset metadata, and practical lossless commands. One physical page may contain chunks' starts, with recovery metadata persisted in out-of-band storage. The authors cite commercially deployed SSD-controller ASIC engines at up to 11/14 GB/s compression/decompression and +5% controller area; these numbers are not an AiF die-level decoder or per-plane area estimate. [Author repository](https://github.com/yingjia-wang/CCZNS) is linked from the paper; its functionality was not executed here.
- [Shin et al., Electronics Letters 2018](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/el.2017.3912) explains conventional same-die multiplane operations require matching operation type and matching block/page location, and studies matching vs wasting pages. Therefore co-scheduling arbitrary ready expert pages on currently unused planes cannot automatically be assumed. A device that relaxes address alignment requires a supported NAND-specific command or a new explicitly costed die mechanism.

## 2026-11-02 21:16 UTC — a genuine unresolved integration question, not a result

- **Do not equate known building blocks with a proof of impossibility.** One potentially nontrivial resource conflict remains: in a single same-WL multi-reference operation, can the first completed logical weight page be captured, ECC-corrected and fed into the AiF PE *while* remaining comparisons proceed, with both input-vector latches retained? A valid proposal would specify latch-by-latch lifetimes and ports, sense/reference ordering, arbitration, ECC latency/throughput, PE/FIFO scheduling, page addresses, and ordinary-read discharge at command end. The multiple-latch / joint-command patents do not resolve this particular AiF streaming schedule, and the cited multi-reference digital-MAC patent does not provide its measured tR, area or retained-vector design.
- The cleanest exact control uses the two logical pages for two raw INT8 byte-stream page payloads. If each output row fits in a page and rows are kept whole, the two PE phases may produce disjoint output rows without bit-plane reconstruction or new cross-page partial-sum semantics; when a row spans pages, account for accumulation and fixed-reference arithmetic. If early page completion overlaps later sensing, the available slack depends on the *actual* read ordering and complete ECC/PE path, not just the final measured single-page tR.
- For a grouped-precision variant, deciding after a joint read to discard the low-significance page does not save its already incurred sensing. A true coarse-only action must be decided before scheduling the expensive read and compared with ordinary LSB-only data at matched quality. The full-precision path must also beat the simpler two-raw-page control. Physical data are presently insufficient for a credible latency/area claim, so recommend retaining this as speculative prior map material, not starting a formal candidate/experiment solely on paper timing.
- **Interim decision, both pools:** the existing references heavily constrain new claims, but no reviewed source proves all possible small-hardware integration solutions are dominated. The pseudoMLC/one-shot path lacks jointly measured tR, physical state/ECC retention evidence, concrete AiF latch allocation and a matched ordinary-read baseline; the entropy path lacks an identified new wave-aware mechanism and validated INT8, small-area throughput at the correct frame/layout. A later formal candidate should state the precise new resource conflict and a testable architecture with predeclared stopping criteria. No experiment, candidate implementation, or model data was accessed in this screen.

- **Sense lower bound with explicit assumptions:** for four ordered analog cell states, comparisons against fixed wordline voltages shared across cells and producing one binary result per cell split the voltage axis into at most `k+1` distinguishable intervals after `k` distinct thresholds. Distinguishing all four states under that model needs at least three distinct thresholds. [Li et al. §II](https://sites.ecse.rpi.edu/~tzhang/pub/UECTCASII2014.pdf) gives the corresponding three-sense example. The claim does not prohibit different circuit primitives such as independently adaptive per-cell decisions or multibit/ramp ADC outputs; those need separate hardware and characterization. A command returning two pages is not evidence that it used one binary comparison.
